# Bond imports

Settings → Bonds · CDSL CAS → enter the first holder’s PAN → Enable bond imports. The PAN is encrypted server-side and used only to decrypt statement PDFs. Sync Gmail documents imports all enabled sources, including CAS and Wint emails through the single bonds source.

CAS defines current held ISINs, quantities and dated values. The initial parser supports the inspected CDSL layout with one NSDL account containing NCDs; account summaries, closing quantities, prices and the complete Debts allocation must reconcile. Unsupported or inconsistent statements require review and preserve the last successful balances. A newer complete snapshot replaces prior holdings; explicit zero-debt / Nil Holding statements clear them. Older statements cannot regress balances. Equities, mutual funds and transaction rows are excluded.

Authenticated Wint purchase and payout emails enrich CAS holdings only. Purchases must match the hashed demat account and predate the statement. Complete matching quantities establish invested cash and purchase accrued interest; a receipt can be corroborated by CAS quantities but cannot independently create a holding. Later purchases and email-only ISINs never add positions. CAS coupon metadata takes precedence; a different Wint quoted rate is shown separately.

Payouts record explicit gross interest, TDS, net interest, principal and next payment dates. Imported totals may be incomplete and are not lifetime returns. Small reported rounding differences remain visible; larger inconsistencies require review. Payments before the matched ownership window cannot redeem a current lot. Only explicit full redemption after the CAS date, with complete matched purchases and fully repaid nominal principal, removes a stale holding. Partial or unreconciled repayments retain the CAS value and show a reconciliation note. Proceeds are never automatically added as cash.

Net worth uses dated CAS values rather than estimated prices, maturity proceeds or purchase cost. Actual invested cash is displayed separately; daily gains, accrued returns and unrealized P&L are not inferred. Projected value at maturity compounds each fully matched, confirmed purchase’s invested cash at its email YTM from settlement to maturity (ACT/365, annual compounding), then sums the lots. It assumes interim payouts are reinvested at that YTM, before tax; it is neither the final bond payment nor today’s net worth. Missing YTM or unconfirmed settlement leaves the projection unavailable. A common YTM is shown only when all matched lots agree. Migration 0018 re-scans imported Wint emails in bounded batches to enrich legacy events without duplicating cash flows. Unconfirmed passed maturity dates retain holdings and flag a check.

Migrations 0016 and 0017 store owner-scoped settings, hashed accounts, snapshots and import ledgers. Successful snapshot/event writes and import bookkeeping are atomic via database triggers; ledger failures roll back financial mutations. Purchase receipts upgrade to confirmed settlements without double counting. Full demat identifiers and PAN are excluded from financial snapshots and events. Decrypted PDFs are processed in memory. Wint discovery paginates in batches of five to remain within the free Worker database query budget; review errors preserve valid imports and existing holdings.

Validation covers sender/DMARC checks, parsing and arithmetic, decryption, deduplication, receipt upgrades, account isolation, snapshot replacement, cancellation, atomic rollback, ownership windows and query limits. Private validation against the original August CAS and 54 Wint emails reconciled eight current holdings with matched purchase amounts and one subsequent full redemption. Personal source documents are not committed as fixtures.
